Project filters

Blank = unlimited. Depth: 1 = only the post's direct comments, 2 = also their replies, 3 = one level deeper. Max comments: every post keeps up to N of its comments + replies on its own — one post never uses up another's allowance, and the post itself never counts. In 500-target projects, targets and the replies around them always stay visible and never count toward the max. Existing labels are kept. Exports include saved labels or the full source, as selected.
